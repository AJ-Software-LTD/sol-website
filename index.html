What it does

Real positions

Calculated from NASA JPL's orbital elements, for any moment from 3000 BC to 3000 AD.

Travel through time

Speed time up to a year a second, pause, go back to now, or jump to any date.

Notable dates

The tightest planetary alignment in 6,000 years, the 1769 transit of Venus, the impact that ended the dinosaurs, and more.

A living Sun

Solar flares, asteroids on smooth, gravity-bent paths, and the occasional supernova. Earth is never hit.

Made for the Mac

A menu bar app for every display and desktop. About 1% of one processor core in normal use.

Private

No account, no ads, no tracking. SOL collects no data at all.
